Frequently asked questions

What is a word counter?

A word counter measures how many words appear in your text.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting text on whitespace and removing punctuation and symbols from each token.

Are hyphenated words counted as one word?

Hyphenated terms are treated as a single word in this tool.

Are numbers counted as words?

Yes, numeric tokens are counted as words when they appear as separate text terms.

Does character count include spaces?

Character count with spaces includes blank spaces. The tool also shows a separate count without spaces.

Is pasted text uploaded or stored online?

No. Your text and notes remain in your browser unless you export them manually.
